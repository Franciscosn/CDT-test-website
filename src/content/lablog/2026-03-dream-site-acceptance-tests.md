The day-one detectors for DREAM, the diffraction instrument for materials research at the European Spallation Source in Lund, have passed their site acceptance tests.

The detector system was designed and built by CDT in Heidelberg. It uses our JALOUSIE technology: boron-10 coated cathodes instead of helium-3, read out by **CDT’s own electronics**. The detector covers a large solid angle around the sample.

With the acceptance tests complete, the detectors are ready for commissioning with neutrons.
